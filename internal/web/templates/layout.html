What counts as a spike?

Positive charges per provider and service must exceed the previous seven covered days’ average by more than 50% and at least 20 {{.Currency}}. The candidate day is excluded. A new service can trigger an alert against a zero baseline. Credits do not hide positive-charge spikes.

A shared billing format

Convert your AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud export to these five columns.

date,provider,service,cost,currency
  • date uses YYYY-MM-DD, one billing day per row.
  • provider is aws, azure, or gcp.
  • service is the service name.
  • cost is a decimal with up to six places. Credits are negative.
  • currency is USD, EUR, or GBP. One currency per file.
